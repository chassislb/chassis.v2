This Privacy Policy explains what information Chassis may collect through this website, how that information may be used, and how third-party platforms may be involved depending on the interaction, project, or technical setup.

POLICY OVERVIEW

Last updated: May 20, 2026

Chassis collects only the information needed to communicate, understand inquiries, and improve the website or project experience.

Chassis is a business structuring, operations systems, and execution infrastructure brand. This website may collect information when visitors contact Chassis, submit inquiries, book calls, request services, or interact with website features.

Information may also be processed through third-party platforms depending on the website, project scope, client requirements, hosting setup, communication method, analytics setup, payment structure, database configuration, or technical implementation.

Chassis does not sell personal information.

INFORMATION THIS POLICY COVERS

Simple, practical, and relevant to this website and client inquiries.

01
Contact Information

Name, email address, phone number, business name, project details, inquiry details, or any information submitted directly through forms, calls, messages, or email.

02
Website Usage Data

Basic analytics such as pages visited, device type, browser, approximate location, traffic source, and how visitors interact with the website.

03
Project & Scheduling Data

Information shared when booking a call, discussing a project, requesting a proposal, submitting business details, or communicating about services.

04
Technical Data

Technical information that may be processed through hosting, analytics, databases, forms, automations, security tools, browser behavior, or project-related platforms.

The information collected is used to respond, improve, operate, and deliver the work properly.
