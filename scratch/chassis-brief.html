Founder & Market

Sophia Ayoubi, founder. 8+ years restructuring hospitality venues, launching startups, and building execution systems. Operational background at Emirates Airlines before this work. Based in Batroun, Lebanon.

Market: Lebanon and the GCC (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman). Work is delivered remotely.

Audience: founders and operators running active but unstructured businesses, mainly hospitality, startups, and consulting practices, but not limited to them.

Good fit: real activity with no clear offer, or a business running on friction and inconsistency. Not a fit: surface-level fixes, content or ads without structural work, or an idea with no discipline to follow a system.
